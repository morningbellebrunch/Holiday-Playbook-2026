How we roll out
Practice makes it smooth
  1. Share the features early. Playbooks are available to both BOH and FOH.
  2. Cover the features in preshift.
  3. Walk through the printed builds the week before Go Live.
  4. Try the features daily, send photos, and share feedback.
  5. Ring in every practice item and comp it under “Training”.
  6. Run a fun weekend contest with prizes.
  7. Use features for Lobby Comfort, Surprise and Delight, and special occasions.

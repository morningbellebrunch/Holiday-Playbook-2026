Line Builds

Tap a dish for portions and the build in order.

Ingredients
  • 1 fl ozLiquid butter
  • 1 eachEnglish muffin
  • 1 portionPulled pot roast, 2 oz
  • 1 ozCaramelized onions
  • 1 ozMushrooms, sauteed
  • 1 ozSwiss cheese, shredded
  • 2 eachPoached eggs
  • 1.5 fl ozHollandaise
  • 1 eachScallions, thin sliced
  • 1 eachSmoked paprika
  • Served withCherry tomato salad
Build
  1. Toast the English muffin on the flat top with liquid butter.
  2. Heat the pulled pot roast, caramelized onions, and sauteed mushrooms on the flat top.
  3. Cook two poached eggs while the pot roast mixture heats.
  4. When the mixture is hot, add the Swiss cheese on top and steam with water to melt it.
  5. Move the griddled English muffin to the plate and top with the cheesy pot roast mixture.
  6. Top with the poached eggs.
  7. Finish with hollandaise, scallions, and paprika.
  8. Serve with a cherry tomato salad.
Ingredients
  • 1 fl ozLiquid butter
  • 1 eachBiscuit, precooked
  • 2 ozHam, shredded
  • .75 ozBacon, cooked and chopped
  • 1 ozCaramelized onions
  • .75 ozPoblano peppers, roasted
  • .75 ozRoasted corn
  • 3 ozSignature potatoes
  • 3 ozQueso blanco
  • 1 eachScallions, thin sliced
  • Served with2 eggs, cooked to order
Build
  1. Split the biscuit and heat both halves on the flat top with liquid butter.
  2. Heat the shredded ham, chopped bacon, caramelized onions, roasted corn, and poblano peppers.
  3. Heat the signature potatoes separately.
  4. Cook the eggs as ordered.
  5. Pile the potatoes on the plate and set the biscuit halves on top.
  6. Top the biscuit halves with the ham, bacon, onion, corn, and pepper mixture.
  7. Top with the heated queso blanco.
  8. Add the eggs to the plate and finish with scallions.
Ingredients
  • 2 each2 oz thin pancakes
  • 2 blue handle dishTriple chocolate cheesecake cannoli filling, 2.5 oz
  • 1 ozChocolate sauce
  • 1 ozWhipped cream
  • .25 ozChocolate chips
  • 1 eachMaraschino cherry
  • 1 eachPowdered sugar garnish
  • 1 eachMint sprig garnish
Build
  1. Butter the griddle and make two 2 oz pancakes. Spread them out to keep them thin.
  2. Pull the pancakes off the griddle and set them top side down on a clean cutting board.
  3. Place one blue handle scoop of chocolate filling in the center of each pancake.
  4. Roll the pancakes and cross them on the entree plate.
  5. Place the whipped cream where the pancakes cross.
  6. Drizzle with chocolate sauce and sprinkle with chocolate chips.
  7. Garnish with the maraschino cherry, powdered sugar, and mint.
Ingredients
  • 1 fl ozLiquid butter
  • 2 eachBrioche slices, Michigan Bread
  • 3 fl ozFrench toast batter
  • 3 ozCinnamon roasted apples, chopped
  • 1 ozBacon, cooked and chopped
  • .75 fl ozCaramel syrup
  • .75 fl ozVanilla glaze
  • .1 ozPure maple granules
  • 1 eachPowdered sugar garnish
  • 1 eachMint sprig garnish
Build
  1. Make two slices of French toast from the thin cut brioche on the flat top.
  2. Heat the chopped apples and bacon on the flat top. Steam the mixture with water.
  3. Cut the French toast on an angle and shingle it on the plate.
  4. Top with the heated apple and bacon mixture.
  5. Drizzle with caramel sauce and vanilla glaze.
  6. Sprinkle with the maple granules.
  7. Garnish with powdered sugar and mint.
Ingredients
  • 1 fl ozLiquid butter
  • 1 eachHerb focaccia sandwich bread
  • 1 portionPulled pot roast, 2 oz
  • 1 ozRoasted red peppers
  • 1 ozSwiss cheese, shredded
  • 1 eachFried egg
  • .5 ozCrispy onions
  • .5 fl ozChipotle aioli
  • Serve with2.5 fl oz au jus
  • Side4 oz signature potatoes or 1 cherry tomato salad
Build
  1. Heat both sides of the focaccia on the flat top with liquid butter.
  2. Heat the pot roast and roasted red peppers on the flat top.
  3. Fry an egg on the flat top or in a nonstick pan while the pot roast heats. Shape it into a rectangle.
  4. Place the cheese on top of the pot roast and steam.
  5. Set the focaccia on the cutting board and drizzle with chipotle aioli.
  6. Place the fried egg on the bottom half of the focaccia.
  7. Top the egg with the pot roast mixture.
  8. Top with crispy onions, then the other focaccia half.
  9. Cut the sandwich on an angle and place it on the plate.
  10. Set a small white fruit dish behind the sandwich with a bell creamer of hot au jus.
  11. Place the ordered side in the remaining space.
Plating

Au jus arrives in a 2.5 oz bell creamer inside an empty white fruit dish. The server or food runner pours it into the dish tableside, then removes the creamer.

Ingredients
  • 1 fl ozLiquid butter
  • 6 fl ozEgg whites
  • 1 portionChicken, diced, 2 oz
  • .20 ozBaby spinach
  • 4 slicesMarinated tomato
  • 2 slicesFresh mozzarella
  • .75 fl ozBasil pesto
  • .15 fl ozBalsamic glaze
  • Served with1 slice buttered toast, guest’s choice
Build
  1. Heat the liquid butter in a nonstick omelet pan, then add the egg whites.
  2. Cook the egg whites two thirds of the way, draining uncooked egg under the omelet.
  3. Cover with baby spinach, three marinated tomato slices, the diced chicken, and two slices of mozzarella. Heat on the bottom shelf of the salamander until the toppings are hot.
  4. Remove from the salamander and fold onto the plate.
  5. Place the last marinated tomato slice on top of the folded omelet.
  6. Drizzle with pesto and balsamic glaze.
  7. Serve with the buttered toast.
Ingredients
  • 2 eachHot cocoa packets
  • 8 fl ozHot water
  • 2 ozWhipped cream
  • 10 eachChocolate chips
  • .25 fl ozCaramel syrup
  • 1 eachMaraschino cherry
Build
  1. Empty the cocoa packets into the coffee cup and fill with hot coffee water, leaving room for the whipped cream.
  2. Stir until the cocoa dissolves.
  3. Top with a whipped cream swirl.
  4. Finish with chocolate chips, caramel sauce, and the maraschino cherry.

Ingredients
  • 1 eachCheese sauce bag, 80 oz
  • 12 ozCheddar cheese, shredded
  • 2 cupsHot water
Steps
  1. Thaw the cheese sauce bag in the walk in for 2 days, or under cold running water.
  2. Place the cheese sauce and hot water in a heavy bottom pan.
  3. Slowly heat to a simmer, stirring constantly so it does not burn.
  4. At a simmer, stir in the cheddar and keep stirring until it melts.
  5. Remove from the heat and transfer to a hotel pan to cool.
  6. Once cool, transfer to 1/6 pans.
StorageCooler or steamwell
Container1/6 pan
Shelf life6 days
Ingredients
  • 7 eachChocolate cheesecake slices
Steps
  1. Thaw the cheesecake slices in the fridge overnight. Make sure they are fully thawed.
  2. Place all the thawed cheesecake in the Robot Coupe bowl.
  3. Pulse until smooth, scraping the sides with a spatula. Do not over puree or it turns runny.
  4. Transfer to a 1/6 pan for service.

Ingredients
  • 2 ozPulled pot roast
Steps
  1. Thaw the pot roast in the fridge for 3 to 4 days, until fully thawed.
  2. Remove it from the bag. Discard the liquid and fat chunks in the trash, never down the drain.
  3. Break the pot roast into small chunks, removing any fat.
  4. Pull all the meat apart by hand.
  5. Portion into 2 oz bags.
  6. Freeze any extra portions you do not need.
